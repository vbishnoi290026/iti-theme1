About Institute

A leading government technical institute dedicated to skill development and vocational excellence in Rajasthan.

Govt. ITI Chhattargarh Bikaner is a Government Industrial Training Institute located in Bikaner, Rajasthan, providing high-quality vocational and technical education.

The institute offers multiple technical and non-technical trades designed to help students build practical skills and become job-ready professionals.

Through industry-focused learning, experienced trainers, and hands-on workshops, students gain real-world experience that prepares them for successful careers.
